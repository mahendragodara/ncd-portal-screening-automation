## 📌 About NCD Screening Automation

**NCD Screening Automation** is a specialized browser extension created for healthcare professionals, **Community Health Officers (CHO)**, **Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANM)**, **Staff Nurses**, and **Data Entry Operators** working on the **National NCD Portal (Ministry of Health and Family Welfare - MoHFW, Govt of India)** (`https://ncd.mohfw.gov.in/`).

Entering hundreds of non-communicable disease (NCD) screening records, CBAC checklists, and patient registrations manually takes hours of tedious data entry. This extension enables health teams to prepare records in an Excel sheet and **automatically populate and submit all screening forms in seconds with 100% accuracy**.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

- ⚡ **10x Faster Bulk Entry:** Fills a complete screening record (Hypertension, Diabetes, Oral, Breast & Cervical Cancer) in under 3 seconds per patient.
- 📋 **Bulk Excel Upload:** Upload your daily or monthly Excel sheet (`.xlsx`) directly into the extension popup.
- 🆔 **New Enrollment & Existing Patient Screening:** Supports both new patient registrations with ABHA ID generation and screening updates for existing patients.
- 🛡️ **100% Client-Side & Secure:** Operates locally inside your authorized browser session without storing patient medical records on external servers. Fully compliant with MoHFW guidelines.
- 📊 **Live Progress Tracker:** Real-time visual progress counter showing completed, pending, and skipped entries.
- 🔄 **Smart Error Handling:** Automatically handles slow server responses, re-attempts timed-out requests, and highlights records with missing mandatory fields.
- 📱 **Dedicated Support & WhatsApp Community:** Instant updates, video tutorials, and technical support.

## 📥 How to Use (Step-by-Step Guide)

### Step 1: Install the Extension
Install the official extension from the Chrome Web Store:
> **👉 [Install from Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/)**

### Step 2: Download the Official Excel Template
Download the pre-formatted Excel template designed specifically for the extension:
> **📥 [Download NCD_Patient_Data_Template.xlsx](./NCD_Patient_Data_Template.xlsx)**

Fill in your patient details (Name, Age, Gender, Mobile, BP, Blood Sugar, CBAC checklist questions) into the template.

### Step 3: Login to NCD Portal
Open the official portal in your Google Chrome or Brave browser:
👉 **[https://ncd.mohfw.gov.in/portal/](https://ncd.mohfw.gov.in/portal/)**
Log in to your Facility, Sub-Center (HWC), or PHC account.

### Step 4: Run Automation
1. Open the **NCD Screening Automation** extension from your Chrome toolbar.
2. Upload your prepared Excel file.
3. Click **Start Automation**. The extension will search each patient, auto-fill the forms, and submit sequentially!
